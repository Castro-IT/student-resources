Tips

Effective Study Strategies

Quick methods to get the most out of your study sessions.

  • 1. Active Recall: Test yourself frequently rather than passively re-reading notes.
  • 2. Spaced Repetition: Review topics over several days instead of cramming the night before.
  • 3. Study Groups: Form weekly groups to quiz each other and discuss tricky concepts.
  • 4. Use Tutoring: Visit campus tutoring centers early in the term before exam week.
