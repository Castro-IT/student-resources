Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about courses, schedules, campus tech, and advising.

Where can I find syllabus documents for my courses? ▾

Course syllabi are posted on your campus LMS portal at the start of each semester. You can also download our generic Syllabus Template.

How do I book an appointment with academic advisors? ▾

Advising appointments can be booked through the student portal or by visiting Room 204 in the Student Center. Walk-in hours are Monday to Thursday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Are study guides free to download? ▾

Yes! All study guides and review packets on this portal are free for enrolled students. Visit the Study Guides page to download materials.

How do I connect to campus Wi-Fi? ▾

Select Campus-Secure on your device and log in with your primary student ID and password. Accept the security certificate when prompted.
